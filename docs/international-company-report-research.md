# International Company Analysis Report Research

## Findings

CFA Institute describes a company research report as covering historical and current financials, industry and competitors, forecasts of future financial statements, valuation, recommendation and risks. It also emphasizes business-model understanding and identifying revenue, profitability, cash-flow and financial-position drivers.

Wall Street Prep describes a full equity research report as commonly including investment recommendation, key takeaways, quarterly update, catalysts and financial exhibits. It distinguishes a short note from a comprehensive initiation report.

Financial Edge Training states that a full report commonly includes front-page security information, investment summary, business description, industry and competitive positioning, valuation, financial analysis and forecasts, investment risks and ESG. It also emphasizes target price/recommendation context and earnings-quality review.

## Selected form for ORCA

The selected form is a **research-grade company analysis report**, combining CFA analytical coverage with the practical sections used in international equity research initiation reports:

1. Report header and recommendation snapshot.
2. Executive investment summary and thesis.
3. Business model and revenue drivers.
4. Industry structure and competitive positioning.
5. Historical financial performance and earnings quality.
6. Forecasts, assumptions and scenario analysis.
7. Valuation using explicit methods and sensitivity.
8. Catalysts and thesis-monitoring events.
9. Risks, invalidation conditions and mitigants.
10. ESG and governance considerations.
11. Conclusion, disclosures, sources and data-confidence metadata.
